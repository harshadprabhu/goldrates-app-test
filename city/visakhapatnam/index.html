FAQs

What is the gold rate today in Visakhapatnam?

The rate shown above is computed live from international spot gold prices and refreshes automatically about once a minute. It reflects the same national jewellers shown on MyGoldRates, plus jewellers serving the south region that covers Visakhapatnam.

Which jewellers are compared for Visakhapatnam?

We compare national jewellery chains available across India alongside regional jewellers serving Andhra Pradesh. The comparison list above is sorted lowest to highest per gram.

Is GST included in the Visakhapatnam gold rate?

No, rates are shown pre-GST by default. Tap +3% GST to add it to every price shown on this page.

Does the rate in Visakhapatnam differ from other cities?

Gold's underlying spot price is national, so the base rate is the same everywhere in India. What differs city to city is which jewellers are active locally and their individual premiums — this page highlights jewellers relevant to Visakhapatnam specifically.
